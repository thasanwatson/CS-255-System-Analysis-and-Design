# CS-255-System-Analysis-and-Design

## DriverPass Project Reflection

**1. Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?**

The client for this project was DriverPass, a company that wanted to help students prepare for their driving tests. They needed a system that would allow students to take online practice tests, schedule driving lessons, and track their progress. The system also needed to allow employees to manage appointments, student information, and other administrative tasks. My job was to analyze the client's requirements and design a system that would meet their needs.

**2. What did you do particularly well?**

I think I did particularly well at organizing the requirements and breaking them down into different parts of the system. Creating the business requirements document helped me understand what DriverPass needed before moving into the design process. I also think I did well with the UML diagrams because they helped me visualize how the different users and system components would interact.

**3. If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?**

If I could revise one part of my work, I would focus on improving my UML diagrams. While I was able to show the main functions and interactions, I think some of the diagrams could be more detailed and easier to follow. I would spend more time reviewing the relationships between objects and making sure each diagram clearly represents the system's requirements.

**4. How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?**

I interpreted the user's needs by reviewing the DriverPass interview transcript and identifying the main features the client wanted. I used that information to develop the business requirements and create a system design that supported features like scheduling lessons, taking practice exams, and managing student accounts. Considering the user's needs is important because even a well-designed system won't be successful if it doesn't solve the problems the users actually have.

**5. How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?**

When designing software, I think it's important to understand the problem before jumping into development. I would start by gathering requirements, identifying the users, and determining what the system needs to accomplish. From there, I would use tools like UML diagrams, process models, and object models to organize the design and understand how everything connects. One thing this course taught me is that taking the time to plan and review a system early on can prevent a lot of problems later in development.
